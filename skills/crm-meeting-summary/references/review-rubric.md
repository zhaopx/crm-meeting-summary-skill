# Review Rubric

Review priority order:
1. fact accuracy
2. risk coverage
3. business value

## Pass conditions

### Fact accuracy
- all major claims trace back to source evidence
- scenario classification is supportable
- no fabricated CRM or memory facts
- human-readable and machine-readable outputs are consistent

### Risk coverage
- major risks implied by evidence or knowhow are surfaced
- risk level is not understated
- policy or compliance boundaries are not ignored

### Business value
- summary is actionable
- next actions are specific
- knowhow focus items are explicitly addressed
- missing information is visible

## Fail examples
- customer intent asserted without evidence
- opportunity stage described but no source supports it
- escalation signals present in notes but omitted in output
- summary looks polished but gives no next action

## Regeneration guidance rule
When failing review, provide repair instructions only for failed dimensions. Keep them concrete and evidence-oriented.
