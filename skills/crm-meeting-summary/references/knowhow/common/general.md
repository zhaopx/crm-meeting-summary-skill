# Common Knowhow

Apply these rules across all CRM meeting summaries.

## Universal focus points
1. What business objective did this meeting actually serve?
2. What evidence shows progress versus politeness?
3. What risks were stated, implied, or avoided?
4. What commitment was made, by whom, and by when?
5. What information is still missing for a safe next step?

## Universal summary boundaries
- distinguish fact from interpretation
- mark uncertainty explicitly
- avoid over-claiming customer intent
- avoid claiming internal certainty when stakeholder ownership is unclear

## Universal action rule
Recommended next actions must be tied to evidence from this meeting or validated CRM context.
