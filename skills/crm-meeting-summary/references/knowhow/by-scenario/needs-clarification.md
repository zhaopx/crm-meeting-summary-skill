# Scenario Knowhow: 需求澄清

## Focus points
- Is the underlying business problem real and urgent?
- Are the pain points specific or still generic?
- Are success criteria visible?
- Are blockers, constraints, or dependencies explicit?
- Is there evidence of buying seriousness or only exploratory curiosity?

## Good signals
- concrete current-state pain
- measurable success criteria
- named owners or decision participants
- timeline or consequence of inaction

## Risk signals
- vague requirements without urgency
- no owner for next step
- no link between pain and budget or priority
- solution discussion starts before need is clear

## Suggested next-step logic
Prefer actions that tighten qualification quality: stakeholder mapping, success criteria confirmation, blocker clarification, timeline validation.
